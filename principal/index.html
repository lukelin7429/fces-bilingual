Principal’s Office · 校長室

The Lamplighter 提燈者 — 黃保勝校長

“Seeing a child’s needs is the value of an educator; realising a child’s dreams is our craft.” 「看見孩子的需要，是教育人的價值；成就孩子的夢想，是教育人的專業。」

15th Principal · 第十五任校長

Huang Pao-sheng 黃保勝 校長

Appointed August 2023 (民國 112 年 8 月). A graduate of the National Taichung University of Education (M.Ed., National Education), Taipei Municipal University of Education, and the National Chiayi Normal College. He taught at Xuguang and Heping Elementary, served as section chief and director, and worked at Taichung’s Teacher In-Service Centre and the Information & Network Education Centre — a principal with a deep background in technology, STEAM, and bilingual education.

黃校長畢業於臺中教育大學國民教育研究所、臺北市立師範學院初等教育系、省立嘉義師範專科學校；曾任教烏日旭光國小、南區和平國小，歷任組長、主任，並曾服務於臺中市教師研習中心與資訊教育暨網路中心。是一位深耕科技、STEAM 與雙語教育的校長。

Walking into school, everything is education. 走進學校，一切都是教育

Principal Huang describes the work of a teacher in one image: a lamplighter. Every child deserves a bright future of their own, and the job of an educator is to light the flame of learning and let it shine on the road ahead. He believes the keys are professionalism and passion — using expertise to awaken each child’s many intelligences, and warmth to lift their confidence, so that no child is left behind.

黃校長用一個畫面形容老師的工作：提燈者。每個孩子都值得擁有屬於自己光明燦爛的未來，教育人的任務，就是點燃學習的熱情，照亮孩子前方的路。他相信關鍵在於專業與熱情——以專業啟發孩子的多元智慧，用熱情提升孩子的自信，帶好每一個孩子，一個都不放掉。
“Bring every child along — not only as a duty, but as an achievement.” 帶好每一位孩子，不只是責任，也是一種成就。
Our Direction

Four Commitments

治校四面向
1

Students First, Talent for All學生第一，適性揚才

Treasure every child and let each one shine; awaken many kinds of potential so children can surpass themselves and face the future.

賞識每一個孩子，讓每個孩子都發光；啟發多元潛能，讓孩子超越自己、迎向未來。

2

Explore & Experience體驗探索，涵育素養

Build many kinds of learning spaces and use technology wisely — so that core competencies grow through hands-on, real experience.

建構多元學習場域、善用科技有效教學；以體驗學習，涵育帶得走的核心素養。

3

A Kind Campus友善校園，共榮共好

Respect and care for one another, look after the vulnerable, and lead gently — so the whole school can flourish together.

尊重關懷、照顧弱勢、傳遞正向能量；柔性領導、良性溝通，讓全校共榮共好。

4

Hand in Hand大手拉小手，幸福校園走

Big hands holding small hands — teachers and parents walk together, pooling every resource to build a happy place to learn.

大手拉小手，給孩子溫暖與關懷；親師攜手，整合資源，打造幸福的學習環境。
