How We Do Bilingual

Three Ways English Lives Here

英語在豐洲的三種樣子

Since 2019, FengChou has been an immersive-English special school — not an extra class, but English woven into real moments of the school day, supported by our own foreign teacher. 自 108 學年起，豐洲就是沉浸式英語教學特色學校——不是多上一節課，而是把英語織進校園日常的真實時刻，並有專屬外師協同教學。

Immersive English · 沉浸式

English through Art用藝術課學英語

Our flagship programme weaves English into sixth-grade visual-arts lessons, so children use the language to make and describe real things.

旗艦課程把英語融入六年級視覺藝術課，讓孩子用英語去「做」、去描述真實的作品。

Foreign Teacher · 外師

A Real Voice in the Room教室裡真實的聲音

A foreign English teacher co-teaches on campus, giving children a real, everyday reason to speak English with a native speaker.

外籍英語老師在校協同教學，讓孩子有真實、日常的理由，與母語人士開口說英語。

Every Day · 日常

Word of the Day & Air Time每日一字與雙語廣播

每日一字短片、教室英語、雙語廣播——讓英語流動在最平常的校園時光裡。

Word of the Day · 每日一字

Seven Words from Our Campus

七個來自校園的英文字

Seven words from FengChou life — each with its own video and two example sentences, in English and Chinese. 七個來自豐洲校園生活的英文字——每個字都有自己的影片，和兩句中英對照例句。

Word 01
band (n.)
樂隊
The school band plays music at the anniversary show.
學校樂隊在校慶表演演奏音樂。
I want to join the band and learn the trumpet.
我想加入樂隊，學吹小號。
Word 02
exercise (v.)
運動
We exercise on the field every morning.
我們每天早上在操場運動。
Exercising every day keeps us healthy and strong.
每天運動讓我們健康又強壯。
Word 03
rehearsal (n.)
排練
The dance team has a rehearsal after class.
舞蹈隊放學後有一場排練。
We need one more rehearsal before the big show.
大表演前，我們還需要再排練一次。
Word 04
dance (n./v.)
跳舞
The children dance happily on the stage.
孩子們在舞台上開心地跳舞。
Our class will perform a dance at the school party.
我們班會在學校晚會表演一支舞。
Word 05
running (v.)
跑步
Running makes our tug-of-war team stronger.
跑步讓我們的拔河隊更強壯。
The students are running around the track.
學生們正在操場跑道上跑步。
Word 06
session (n.)
活動 / 一場
We had a fun games session on Children’s Day.
兒童節我們有一場好玩的遊戲活動。
The morning session starts at nine o’clock.
上午的活動九點開始。
Word 07
school anniversary (n.)
校慶
Our school anniversary is a big celebration every year.
我們的校慶，每年都是一場盛大的慶祝。
Students sing and dance on the school anniversary.
學生在校慶這天唱歌、跳舞。
